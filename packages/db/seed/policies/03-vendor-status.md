# Vendor Status Policy

## Active vendors
Invoices from vendors with status "active" may proceed to PO matching and, if all checks pass, to a payment proposal.

## Suspended vendors
Vendors with status "suspended" are under review by Finance (for example, for a compliance or banking-details issue). Invoices from suspended vendors must be escalated to a human immediately. No payment may be proposed, regardless of PO match.

## Pending vendors
Vendors with status "pending" have not finished onboarding (bank details or tax documents not yet verified). Invoices from pending vendors must be escalated with the reason "vendor onboarding incomplete".

## Unknown vendors
If the vendor named on the invoice cannot be found in the vendor register, the invoice must be escalated. The agent must never create a new vendor or contact an email address taken from the invoice itself, because this is a common fraud pattern.
