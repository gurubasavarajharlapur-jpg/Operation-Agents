# Payment Approval Authority Policy

## Every payment needs a human
No payment is ever released automatically. The agent may only propose a payment; a named person must approve it in the operations dashboard. The approver's identity and the time of approval are recorded permanently.

## Approval thresholds
Payments up to 10,000.00 (in any currency) can be approved by any member of the operations team. Payments above 10,000.00 and up to 50,000.00 must be approved by a finance manager, and the proposal must say so. Payments above 50,000.00 must not be proposed by the agent; they are escalated to the finance director.

## Rejected proposals
If a human rejects a proposed payment, the case is escalated with the rejection reason. The agent must not re-propose the same payment for the same case.
