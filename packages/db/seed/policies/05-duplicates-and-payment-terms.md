# Duplicate Invoices and Payment Terms Policy

## Duplicate invoices
The same invoice number from the same vendor must only be paid once. If an invoice number has already been received for that vendor, the new invoice must be escalated as a suspected duplicate, even if the amount differs.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are 30 days from the issue date. An invoice with a due date shorter than 14 days from issue is flagged in the proposal so the approver can prioritise it. An invoice that is already past its due date when received is still processed normally, but the proposal must state that it is overdue.

## Early payment
Payment must not be released before the due date unless the invoice offers an early-payment discount, in which case the proposal must state the discount and the deadline.
